What an AI receptionist costs, with the math shown plainly

November 2, 2026 · Ringception AI

The price of an AI receptionist should fit on an index card. No tiers you have to decode, no per-seat math, and no surprise line at the bottom of the bill. Here is every part of what Ringception costs, in plain numbers, so you can work out your own month before you spend a dollar.

← Back to the blog

Most service prices are hard to pin down on purpose. You get a starting figure, a few add-ons you only hear about later, and a first bill that looks nothing like the quote. A receptionist should not work that way. Answering your phone is a simple job to price, so the price should be simple to read.

Ringception has three parts: what it costs to start, what it costs each month, and what it costs when it actually works for you. Here is each part, and then a few worked examples so you can see a real month.

What it costs to start

Starting costs $44. That is $29 for setup plus $15 for your first month. Setup covers building your receptionist around your business: your greeting, your services, the questions it asks callers, and the rules for what it books, what it escalates, and what it simply takes a message on. You hear and approve a test call before it ever answers a real customer.

There is no separate charge for your phone number. The number is included, whether you keep the number your customers already know through forwarding or start with a fresh one.

What it costs each month

After the first month, the service is $15 a month per number. That is the whole base rate. It covers having your receptionist on the line around the clock, every day, including nights, weekends, and holidays, when most small businesses cannot answer at all.

One optional add-on exists. Follow-Up Campaigns, the planned follow-up by text, email, and call that you approve ahead of time, adds $39 a month. Campaigns are available on business lines only, never on personal lines. If you do not want campaigns, you do not pay for them, and nothing else changes.

What usage costs, and why it is separate

On top of the monthly rate, you pay for actual use: calls at 17 cents a minute, billed per second, and texts at 5 cents each. Billed per second matters. A call that lasts just over a minute is charged for the time it used, not rounded up to a full extra minute.

Usage comes out of prepaid credit that you add ahead of time. That design is deliberate. Because the money is already set aside, there is no surprise bill at the end of the month. You can see your balance, top it up when you choose, and your spending can never quietly run past the credit you put in.

A few worked examples

Say your receptionist handles 100 minutes of calls and 40 texts in a month. The calls cost $17.00, because 100 minutes at 17 cents is $17.00. The texts cost $2.00, because 40 texts at 5 cents is $2.00. Your usage total is $19.00, on top of the $15 monthly rate, for $34.00 that month after your first.

A quieter month might be 40 minutes of calls and 10 texts. That is $6.80 in calls and 50 cents in texts, so $7.30 in usage, or $22.30 with the monthly rate. A busier month of 250 minutes and 100 texts comes to $42.50 in calls and $5.00 in texts, so $47.50 in usage, or $62.50 with the monthly rate.

These are illustrations, not promises about your call volume. Your real number depends on how often your phone rings and how long people talk. The point is that you can do this math yourself, on paper, before you start.

What there is not

There is no per-call fee on top of the per-minute rate, no charge for the summary you get after each call, and no fee for nights or weekends. Owner messages are handled plainly too: texts between you and your own agent are included up to the first 150 each month, and 5 cents each past that cap. The full pricing, with honest limits, sits on the pricing page for anyone to read.

What Ringception does about it

Ringception keeps the whole price in the open: $44 to start, then $15 a month per number with the number included, calls at 17 cents a minute billed per second, texts at 5 cents each, all from prepaid credit, and Follow-Up Campaigns at $39 a month more on business lines only if you want them. You approve the voice and the rules on a test call before it answers for real, and every call is reported back to you.
