Your details are already in

Your signup form told us everything: your services, your hours, how you want calls handled, and the name your assistant uses. There is no second form, and nothing else for you to fill out. We review your form and your website, then build your assistant with your name, greeting, and rules.

Want to add something? Reply to your welcome email (on its way to the email you checked out with) and attach any documents there: price lists, service menus, policy sheets. We will fold them into your assistant's answers before your test call.

Signing in to your portal, no password needed

Your portal is where you see your calls, your summaries, and your credit balance. It has no password at all. There is nothing to remember, and nothing to forget.

  1. Go to the portal and type your email address. Use the same email you signed up with.
  2. We email you a sign-in link. It usually lands in under a minute.
  3. Open the email and tap the link. You are in. That is the whole sign-in.

Lost the email, or it is old? Go back to the portal, type your email again, and we send a fresh link. It takes seconds. You stay signed in for 30 days on your own phone or computer, so most days you will not need a link at all.
