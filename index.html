Questions owners ask us

Straight answers, no fine print games.

What happens when a call is urgent?

You tell us what counts as urgent for your business, for example a burst pipe, a no-heat call, or a job over a certain size. When a call matches, your receptionist transfers it straight to your cell phone live. Everything else becomes a detailed summary in your text and email.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Yes. Most owners forward their existing number to the new number we set up, so customers keep calling the number they already know. If you do not have a business number yet, we provide a local one.

What do the call summaries look like?

After every call you get a short message with the caller's name, their phone number, what they need, and anything time-sensitive flagged. It lands by text and email, so nothing sits in a voicemail box you forget to check.

What if a caller asks if it is a robot?

It answers honestly and keeps helping them. In practice, most callers care about two things: that someone answered, and that their message will reach you. Your receptionist makes both of those true.

How does billing work?

The $49 setup is charged once when we start building. The $39 monthly platform fee keeps your number and service active. Talk time is $0.20 per minute, billed per second, totaled at the end of the month and added to your next invoice. You get a monthly minute report with every bill, so you can check our math anytime.

What if my business is seasonal or slows down?

The per-minute model works in your favor. Slow months mean a smaller bill, and your number stays yours the whole time. There is no long-term contract locking you in either way.

How does cancellation work?

Cancel anytime with a simple message to us. Your service runs to the end of the paid period, then the dedicated number is released. No cancellation fees and no retention script gauntlet.
