Good questions

Questions owners ask us

Straight answers, no fine print games.

What happens when a call is urgent?

You decide what counts as urgent, such as a burst pipe, a no-heat call, or a job over a certain size. When a call matches, your receptionist transfers it live to your cell phone. Everything else becomes a detailed summary by text and email.

Can I keep my existing business number?

Yes. Most owners forward their existing number to the new number we set up, so customers keep calling the number they know. If you need a business number, we provide a local one.

What do the call summaries look like?

After every call you get the caller's name, phone number, what they need, and any time-sensitive details flagged. The summary arrives by text and email, not in a voicemail box you might forget to check.

Can my receptionist make calls for me?

Yes, two ways. Text your Ringception number from your registered phone and tell it who to call and why; it confirms with you first, then calls as your business. Or upload a lead sheet in your account and it works the follow-up calls, with a status and summary for every lead. Warm leads only: past customers, inquiries, and people who asked to hear from you. Federal and state law requires their consent to call, and you confirm that consent when you upload.

Are email campaigns and texting campaigns included?

They are an optional add-on for business lines only. Campaigns are not part of the base plan, and they are not available on personal accounts. For $39 a month per business line, the Follow-Up Campaigns add-on runs email and text follow-ups in your business name to your own customers and callers, with the wording approved by you before anything sends. Personal accounts are one-to-one instead: reminders, confirmations, and texts you dictate, never bulk campaigns or follow-up sequences.

What if a caller asks if it is a robot?

It answers honestly and keeps helping. Most callers want to know that someone answered and that their message will reach you. That is what the receptionist is designed to do.

How does billing work?

The $29 setup (our new client promotion) and your first month at $15 are charged when you sign up, $44 total. After that, $15 a month per number keeps your service active, and your number is included. Talk time costs $0.17 per minute, billed per second, and it runs through your Ringception account wallet: you add credit up front, your minutes draw from it, and you can see exactly what you used at any time. We never send a separate talk-time bill.

What if my business is seasonal or slows down?

With per-minute billing, slow months mean lower usage charges. Your number stays yours while the service is active. There is no long-term contract.

How does cancellation work?

Cancel anytime with a simple message. Your service runs to the end of the paid period, then the dedicated number is released. There are no cancellation fees or retention hurdles.
