Features

What your AI receptionist can do

The full list, for your business line or your personal line.

The essentials, handled

A lot more than answering the phone.

The things a small shop actually needs from a receptionist. Nothing bloated.

  • A dedicated local phone number for your business
  • Custom greeting and script using your business name
  • Captures caller name, phone number, and what they need
  • Urgent calls transferred straight to your cell phone
  • Call summary by text and email after every call
  • After-hours, weekend, and holiday coverage, 24/7
  • Answers common questions using your approved information
  • Usage tracking in your Ringception account wallet, so you always know what you used

Answer inbound calls

A friendly 24/7 front desk for questions, lead capture, call summaries, and urgent transfers.

Warm customer outreach

Follow up with customers and opted-in leads about quotes, service reminders, and next steps. No unsolicited cold calls.

Text your receptionist who to call

Need a customer called? Text your Ringception number from your own phone and say who to call and why. Your receptionist confirms by text, then makes the call as your business and sends you the summary.

Upload a warm lead sheet

Put your follow-up list in your account and your receptionist works the calls for you, with a status and a summary for every lead. Warm leads only: past customers, inquiries, and people who asked to hear from you. Federal and state law requires their consent to call.

Calendar coordination

Check availability, request bookings, confirm visits, and send reminders when your calendar is connected.

Promotions and discounts

Tell eligible customers about owner-approved specials and seasonal offers.

Learn from your website

It instantly learns your business and services from your website: your services, hours, FAQs, and service areas. You review and approve the details before it answers a single call.

Keep track of conversations

Capture follow-up needs and send summaries; additional integrations depend on your setup.

This is not a voicemail box and not a press-1 phone tree. Your assistant holds real, natural conversations, asks and answers questions, and gets call tasks done. You decide who it can contact, and it identifies itself honestly as AI when asked.

Text your assistant, it texts back

Text your assistant's number from your own phone, the same way you would text a person. Tell it what you need, ask it a question, hand it a job, or give it an update to deliver, and it texts back with confirmations, answers, and a summary after every call it takes or makes. It is an ongoing two-way conversation, not a one-way command line, so you can reply, follow up, or add a detail whenever it comes to mind.

A real conversation, not a bot

Callers talk with your assistant in plain back-and-forth. It understands what they are asking, asks follow-up questions, and answers with the information you approved. No menus, no phone tree, no script read in a robot voice.

Text it a job, it makes the call

Text its number from your own phone and hand it a call task: call the pharmacy about a refill, book a table, move a dentist appointment, confirm a delivery window, or sit on hold with customer service so you do not have to. It makes the call, handles the conversation, and sends you a summary of how it went.

It books and cancels by phone

Need an appointment set, moved, or cancelled? A reservation changed, a service ended? Your assistant makes the call, handles the hold music and the front desk, and confirms the result back to you by text.

It talks so you do not have to

Some calls you would rather not take yourself: a difficult landlord, a contractor who will not finish the job, or an ex during or after a divorce. Your assistant is the buffer. Polite, factual, and calm, it handles the conversation, passes everything back to you, and only ever says what you approved it to say. It represents you openly as your assistant. It never pretends to be you.

It sends the updates

Text your assistant what needs saying and it delivers the update to the person you name, then handles the reply conversation for you. Confirm the plan, pass along the details, answer the follow-up questions, all in the words you approved, and all reported back to you.

Reminders and check-ins, handled

Appointment confirmations and reminders by call or text, plus optional wake-up or daily check-in calls you set. Ask it to remind, confirm, or follow up, and it reports back when the job is done.

  • You approve the name, the voice, and the greeting before it ever speaks for you
  • Places approved calls for you: bookings, cancellations, confirmations, reminders, and follow-ups
  • Sends the texts you dictate: updates, reminders, and confirmations, and handles the replies

Straight talk

Honest limits, up front

We would rather you know exactly what this is than be surprised later.

It is an AI assistant, and callers can know

It sounds natural and follows your script, but if a caller asks whether it is a real person, it answers honestly. We do not believe in tricking your customers.

It does not take payments

For everyone's safety, it never takes card numbers or payment details over the phone. It captures what the caller needs and passes it to you.

Best at the front door of your business

It works best for messages, intake, common questions, scheduling requests, and urgent transfers. Long negotiations, complex quotes, and sensitive situations need your personal attention.

You stay in control
