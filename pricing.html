ⓘ What is included

Email and text campaigns run for you on your business lines only, on one standard follow-up pipeline. You pick the goal (win back callers who never booked, chase an open estimate, ask for reviews after a finished job, remind customers about appointments, send an update, or run a seasonal offer) and approve the wording before anything sends. Reminders and check-ins go out automatically at the right times, and any reply stops the sequence and hands the conversation back to you. Texts send from your business number and draw from your credit at the usual 5 cents each. Messages between you and your receptionist are included separately from campaigns: the first 150 a month are on us, then 5 cents each. Lists come from your own callers and customers, and you confirm the people on your list agreed to hear from you, as federal law requires. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, texts stop with a STOP reply, and anyone who opts out is removed automatically. Turn it on anytime. After your first month, you can cancel it from your account page, and it keeps working to the end of the paid period. Campaigns are a business lines feature and are not offered on personal accounts.

Texting switches on after a required carrier registration, which can take a few hours. Your calls are answered from day one.
