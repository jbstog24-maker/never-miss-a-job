The service

Ringception AI ("Ringception", "we") provides an AI receptionist service. We build an automated assistant that answers your business phone (or your personal line, on the Personal Assistant plan) in your name, takes caller details and messages, answers questions using information you approve, transfers urgent calls to a number you choose, and sends you a summary after every call. By signing up or using the service, you agree to these terms.

Setup and platform fees

  • Setup is $29, one time, under our new client promotion. It covers building your assistant, tuning it with you, and your approval test call. The setup fee is not refundable once you have approved your assistant on the test call. If we cannot deliver a working receptionist, we refund the setup fee in full.
  • Your first month is the setup fee only. After the first month, the monthly fee is $15 per month for each phone number on your account. It covers the cost of your phone number, keeps your service active, and includes one script tune-up per month.
  • There is no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime.

Prepaid credit

Usage runs on prepaid credit, bought in packs of $20, $50, or $100 through Stripe and added to your Ringception account. Your credit is prepaid: you pay up front, and usage draws it down. Nothing is ever charged to your card without you buying a pack.

  • Talk time: $0.17 per minute, incoming and outgoing, billed per second.
  • Phone numbers are covered by your monthly fee and are never drawn from your credit.
  • Texts: $0.05 per message sent or received, drawn from your credit, if texting is enabled on your account. Optional text packs are a bulk discount: 1,000 texts for $40, 3,000 for $105, or 5,000 for $150. Packed texts are used first, overflow draws from credit at $0.05, and packs never expire while your account is open.
  • When your balance drops under $5, we email you a low-balance alert with top-up links. If your credit runs out, your receptionist pauses automatically at a $2 safety buffer, and it turns itself back on automatically as soon as you top up. A paused account holds your number while the paid period is active.
  • If a monthly payment fails, we will let you know and retry it. If it stays unpaid for 7 days, the service pauses. Your number is held for 30 days after a pause, then released.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Cancel anytime by message or email. No cancellation fee, no retention process.
  • Your service and your dedicated number stay active until the end of the paid period. Please remove any call forwarding pointing at your Ringception number before the period ends; we will remind you 3 days ahead.
  • Any unused credit is your money and is refunded to your original payment method on request when you cancel. Unused text-pack messages have no cash value and are not refundable.
  • There is never a final usage bill. Everything usage-related already drew from your prepaid credit.

It is not a call center or a bulk dialing service. If you regularly need more, we will talk about a higher-capacity arrangement before anything changes.

Directing your assistant by text, and lead lists

Texted instructions are your instructions. When texting is enabled on your account, texts sent to your Ringception number from the phone number you registered with us are treated as instructions from you, with the same authority as instructions you give in your account or by email. Texts from any other number are not acted on as instructions. You are responsible for keeping your registered phone secure, and for telling us right away if that number changes or the phone is lost, so we can update the registration. When you direct your assistant to contact a new person for the first time, it will text you a confirmation before dialing.

Outbound calls and texts under this section draw from your prepaid credit at the standard rates, and how unused credit is handled does not change.

What the AI is, and is not

  • It is an AI assistant. It sounds natural and follows your approved script, but it can make mistakes, and it answers honestly if a caller asks whether it is a real person. It never pretends to be you, or anyone else, as a human.
  • It never takes payments or collects card, bank, or Social Security information.
  • It is not for emergencies. Callers in an emergency should call 911, and your assistant is built to route urgent situations to you, not to manage them.
  • Long negotiations, complex quotes, and sensitive situations belong with you. The assistant captures the details and gets them to you fast.
