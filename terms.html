Terms of Service

The deal, in plain language.

What the service is, what it costs, and what we each owe the other. Last updated October 19, 2026.

The service

Ringception AI ("Ringception", "we") is an AI receptionist and front office service for businesses and individuals. We build an automated assistant that answers your phone line (your business line, or your personal line on the Personal Assistant plan) in your name, holds real conversations with callers, takes caller details and messages, answers questions using information you approve, books appointments, sends texts and emails on your behalf, follows up with your customers, transfers urgent calls to a number you choose, and reports everything it handles back to you, with a summary after every call. By signing up or using the service, you agree to these terms.

Your account

There is no password. You sign in with a link we email you: each link expires after 15 minutes and works one time only, and once you are signed in, your session lasts 30 days. If a link expires, just ask for a fresh one. You are responsible for the activity on your account, for keeping access to your email account secure, and for the accuracy of the business details you provide, since your assistant works from the information you approve.

Setup and platform fees

  • Setup is $29, one time. It covers building your assistant, tuning it with you, and your approval test call. The setup fee is not refundable once you have approved your assistant on the test call. If we cannot deliver a working receptionist, we refund the setup fee in full.
  • There is no free trial. At signup you pay the $29 setup fee plus your first month at $15, $44 total, and billing starts right away. After that, the monthly fee is $15 per month for each phone number on your account, billed on the same day each month. It covers the cost of your phone number, keeps your service active, and includes one script tune-up per month.
  • There is no long-term contract. You can cancel anytime.

Prepaid credit

Usage runs on prepaid credit, bought in packs of $20, $50, or $100 through Stripe and added to your Ringception account. Your credit is prepaid: you pay up front, and usage draws it down. Nothing is ever charged to your card without you buying a pack.

  • Talk time: $0.17 per minute, incoming and outgoing, billed by the exact second, so you pay only for the exact seconds used.
  • Phone numbers are covered by your monthly fee and are never drawn from your credit.
  • Texts: $0.05 per message sent or received, drawn from your credit, if texting is enabled on your account. Messages between you and your agent are treated differently: summaries, alerts, and reminders we send you, your direction texts to your agent, and its replies are included, with the first 150 of those messages each month at no charge and $0.05 each after that. Optional text packs are a bulk discount: 1,000 texts for $40, 3,000 for $105, or 5,000 for $175. Packed texts are used first, overflow draws from credit at $0.05, and packs never expire while your account is open.
  • Texting switches on after a required carrier registration, which can take a few hours. Your calls are answered from day one.
  • When your balance drops under $5, we email you a low-balance alert with top-up links. If your credit runs out, your receptionist pauses automatically at a $2 safety buffer, and it turns itself back on automatically as soon as you top up. A paused account holds your number while the paid period is active.
  • If a monthly payment fails, we will let you know and retry it. If it stays unpaid for 7 days, the service pauses. Your number is held for 30 days after a pause, then released.

Cancellation and refunds

  • Cancel anytime, from inside your account or by message or email. No cancellation fee, no retention process, and we confirm cancellations by email.
  • Your service and your dedicated number stay active until the end of the paid period. Please remove any call forwarding pointing at your Ringception number before the period ends; we will remind you 3 days ahead.
  • Any unused credit is your money and is refunded to your original payment method on request when you cancel. Unused text-pack messages have no cash value and are not refundable.
  • There is never a final usage bill. Everything usage-related already drew from your prepaid credit.

Your responsibilities

  • Give us accurate information at setup (your services, hours, prices you want quoted, transfer rules, and your never-say list), and tell us when it changes. Your assistant can only be as accurate as the information you approve.
  • Approve your assistant on the test call before it goes live. Nothing launches without your approval.
  • If you ask us to call or text anyone on your behalf, or give us a list of people to contact, you confirm you have their consent and the legal right to have us contact them, as laws like the TCPA require. We only contact warm, opted-in leads and people you name, and we honor every opt-out immediately.
  • You are responsible for any recording notice your own callers need under the laws that apply to your business, and for telling us if you want a spoken notice built into your greeting.
  • Fair use: the service is an AI receptionist and intake service for one business location's normal call volume, up to 100 calls per day per number and one call at a time per number. It is not a call center or a bulk dialing service. If you regularly need more, we will talk about a higher-capacity arrangement before anything changes.

Directing your assistant by text, and lead lists

Texted instructions are your instructions. When texting is enabled on your account, texts sent to your Ringception number from the phone number you registered with us are treated as instructions from you, with the same authority as instructions you give in your account or by email. Texts from any other number are not acted on as instructions. You are responsible for keeping your registered phone secure, and for telling us right away if that number changes or the phone is lost, so we can update the registration. When you direct your assistant to contact a new person for the first time, it will text you a confirmation before dialing.

Lead lists: your warranty. If you upload or give us a list of people to contact, you represent and warrant that every person on the list has given prior express consent to be contacted by your business, as the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and state telemarketing laws require; that the list is made up of warm leads (past customers, inquiries, referrals, or people who opted in to hear from your business) and is not a cold, purchased, or scraped list; and that you will tell us immediately about anyone who asks not to be contacted. We contact listed people only during permitted calling hours, the assistant identifies itself honestly as an AI assistant with your business, and we honor every opt-out immediately and record it. We may pause or suspend outbound activity, including a lead list, that generates complaints, shows a spike in opt-outs, or appears non-compliant, and we will tell you if we do. Outbound calls and texts under this section draw from your prepaid credit at the standard rates, and how unused credit is handled does not change.

Follow-Up Campaigns add-on

The terms in this section apply only to business lines with the Follow-Up Campaigns add-on active. Campaigns are not part of the base service, and they are not available on personal accounts. The add-on costs $39 per month per business line and is billed with your subscription from day one; if you add it at signup, it is part of the $83 due that day (the $44 base plus $39), and your monthly total after that is $54. With the add-on, we send email and text follow-ups in your business name to your own customers and callers, as sequences and promotions you approve before anything sends. The consent warranty in the lead lists section above applies to every campaign list: everyone contacted must have agreed to hear from your business. Every campaign email carries an unsubscribe link, every campaign text honors STOP, and opt-outs are removed immediately and permanently. Campaign texts draw from your prepaid credit at the standard text rate. You can cancel the add-on from your account page after your first month; it stays active until the end of the current paid period and is not billed again after that. Personal accounts keep one-to-one texts only, such as reminders, confirmations, and messages you dictate, and those texts are not campaigns.

What the AI is, and is not

  • It is an AI assistant. It sounds natural and follows your approved script, but it can make mistakes, and it answers honestly if a caller asks whether it is a real person. It never pretends to be you, or anyone else, as a human.
  • Calls handled by your assistant may be recorded and summarized. Recordings and summaries are kept for your records and for quality, and your call activity is available in your account.
  • It never takes payments or collects card, bank, or Social Security information.
  • It is not for emergencies. Callers in an emergency should call 911, and your assistant is built to route urgent situations to you, not to manage them.
  • Long negotiations, complex quotes, and sensitive situations belong with you. The assistant captures the details and gets them to you fast.

Acceptable use

Do not use the service for anything unlawful, for cold marketing calls or message blasts, for contacting people who have opted out, or to mislead anyone about who or what is calling. If a use looks non-compliant, we can decline or stop it, and we will tell you why.

We work hard to keep the service up and accurate, but it is provided as is, and we cannot promise it will never miss, mishear, or be briefly unavailable. To the extent the law allows, each side's total liability under these terms is limited to the amount you paid Ringception in the three months before the claim arose, and neither side is liable for indirect losses like lost profits. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law says cannot be limited.
