The Santa Hotline

A live call from the North Pole, just for your child.

Pick Santa Claus, Jingle the Elf, or Rudolph. Tell us your child's name and what they are wishing for. At the time you choose, the phone rings, and they have a real, live conversation. After the call, we email you the recording to keep forever.

One call is $5. Daily check-ins are $20. Every call is up to 3 minutes of pure Christmas magic.
