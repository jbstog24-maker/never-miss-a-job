How your credit works

Prepaid, simple, and always visible

01

It draws down as you talk

Talk time comes out of your credit at $0.17 a minute, billed per second, and texts with your customers are $0.05 each. Messages between you and your receptionist are included: the first 150 a month are on us, then $0.05 each. Your phone number is covered by your $15 monthly fee, never drawn from your credit.

02

We warn you before it runs low

When your credit gets low, we email you with a top-up link while your phones keep working. If it ever runs out, your service pauses and comes right back on automatically as soon as you top up.

03

You can always see it

Your account shows your balance, your talk time, and every call your assistant handled. No surprise bills, ever.

Just topped up after a pause? Your assistant reconnects automatically within about 15 minutes of your payment. If your phones are not back after that, reply to any Ringception email and we will check right away.
